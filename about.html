About Us

Gawade Multispeciality Hospital

A multispeciality healthcare center in Rajgurunagar focused on advanced treatment, compassionate care and broad access to medical services.

Who We Are

Comprehensive care for the local community

Gawade Multispeciality Hospital describes itself as a leading healthcare center in Rajgurunagar, Maharashtra, providing medical and surgical care through expert doctors and modern facilities.

The hospital’s current service mix includes general medicine, surgery, gynecology, pediatrics, diagnostics, emergency care and specialized treatments, supported by ICU services, imaging and pathology.

The hospital also states that it supports affordable, patient-centric care and accepts government health schemes including MJPJAY and CMRF.

Mission
