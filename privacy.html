Privacy

Website Privacy Notice

A simple privacy notice suitable for this static hospital website.

Appointment and career forms

The forms on this website are processed in your browser. When you submit them, the site creates a WhatsApp message and opens WhatsApp for you to review and send. The static website itself does not store form submissions.

Medical information

Do not use the website forms to send sensitive medical records, emergency information or detailed health data. For urgent medical needs, call the hospital directly.

External services

Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, email and telephone services are handled by those providers and are subject to their privacy practices.

Accuracy

Medical services, doctor schedules, insurance empanelment and facilities may change. Confirm important details with the hospital before relying on website information.

Contact

For website or privacy questions, contact gawadehospitalkhed@gmail.com.
